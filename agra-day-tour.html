      Delhi ↔ Agra Route Map

      A private door-to-door same-day journey of approximately 230 km each way, via the Yamuna Expressway — India's finest highway connecting the capital to the Taj Mahal.

      🕌

      Taj Mahal

      The world's greatest monument to love — a UNESCO World Heritage Site built 1631–1653 by Emperor Shah Jahan for his beloved Mumtaz Mahal. Best experienced at sunrise.

      UNESCO SiteSunrise Visit
      🏯

      Agra Fort

      The imposing red sandstone imperial citadel of the Mughal emperors. Home to Shah Jahan's final chapter — the emperor imprisoned yet still gazing at his Taj.

      UNESCO SiteMughal History
      🌿

      Mehtab Bagh

      The 'Moonlit Garden' across the Yamuna — the only spot in Agra offering a perfect reverse panoramic view of the Taj Mahal, as Shah Jahan originally intended.

      The Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) requires monument tickets to be purchased in person at the gate by each visitor with valid ID. Tickets cannot be legally procured in advance by a third party on behalf of guests. Taj Mahal tickets for foreign nationals are approximately ₹1,300/person, Agra Fort is ₹650/person, and Mehtab Bagh is ₹200/person. Our team will guide you through the ticket process on arrival.

      Standard departure is at 04:30 AM from Hotel Godwin Deluxe, Paharganj, New Delhi. This early start is essential to reach the Taj Mahal by sunrise (approximately 07:30–08:00 AM) — the most magical, uncrowded, and photographically stunning time to visit. If you would prefer a later departure (e.g., 06:00 AM), please mention it in your booking notes and we will adjust accordingly, though a sunrise arrival is strongly recommended.

      The complimentary breakfast is served at a curated restaurant in Agra after your Taj Mahal visit (approximately 09:30–10:30 AM). It includes a full North Indian and continental spread: fresh fruit, yoghurt, bread, eggs, paratha or poha, chai or coffee, and fresh juice. Vegetarian, vegan, and Jain options are available — please specify any dietary requirements in your booking notes.

      The Sedan tier provides a private air-conditioned car with a professional chauffeur and breakfast — ideal for budget-conscious travellers.
