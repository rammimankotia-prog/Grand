      Common Questions

      Frequently Asked Questions

      Everything you need to know about the Taj Mahal Express Entry Sunrise Tour — answered by our expert travel curators.

      Sunrise is unanimously considered the most magical time to visit the Taj Mahal. The golden light reflects beautifully off the white marble and the reflecting pool, and the crowds are considerably smaller than later in the day. Our sunrise tour is timed to arrive right at opening so you have the best possible experience.

      Express entry means your tickets are pre-booked and collected in advance, allowing you to use a dedicated fast-track entrance rather than waiting in the standard queue. This saves significant time — especially during peak season when general queues can be 30–60 minutes long.

      Entry fee inclusion depends on the specific package option you select. Our standard package includes the guide fee and transport. An "all-inclusive" option is also available that covers the official Taj Mahal monument entry ticket. Please confirm at the time of booking which package suits your needs.

      Yes — a Delhi pickup option is available. However, due to the 200 km drive from Delhi to Agra (approximately 3 hours), your pickup time from Delhi will be very early (around 2:00–3:00 AM) to reach the Taj Mahal by sunrise. Many guests prefer to stay overnight in Agra for the best experience. We can arrange Agra accommodation as an add-on.

      Photography is freely allowed in the gardens and along the reflecting pool — those are the iconic shots. However, photography inside the mausoleum (the inner chamber) where the actual tombs are located is strictly prohibited. Tripods and camera bags are subject to a separate fee. Mobile phones are permitted for photography throughout the gardens.

      Yes — the Taj Mahal is closed every Friday for general visitors (it is open only to those who wish to offer Friday prayers at the mosque). Please ensure your travel date does not fall on a Friday. Our booking team will automatically flag this and suggest alternatives when you enquire.
