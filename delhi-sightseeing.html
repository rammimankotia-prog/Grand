Route Overview

Delhi Heritage Circuit

A seamless journey connecting the ancient Mughal capital with the grand imperial avenues of New Delhi.

01
🕌

Old Delhi Heritage

Step into the 17th-century Mughal capital of Shahjahanabad. Witness the colossal Jama Masjid, ride a rickshaw through bustling Chandni Chowk, explore Khari Baoli spice market, and see the red sandstone ramparts of Red Fort.

Jama MasjidRickshaw RideSpice Market
02
🏛️

Lutyens' Imperial Center

Drive through the monumental heart of modern India. Admire the solemn arch of India Gate, pay respects at Raj Ghat, and view the stately facades of Rashtrapati Bhavan (Presidential Palace) and Parliament House.

India GateRashtrapati BhavanRaj Ghat
03
🏰

South Delhi Monuments

Trace the remnants of Delhi's medieval empires. Tour the pristine garden-tomb of Emperor Humayun, marvel at the towering 12th-century Qutub Minar complex, and gaze at the modern lotus-shaped temple of the Baha'i faith.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Same Day Delhi Sightseeing Tour — answered by our Delhi heritage experts.

Our standard itinerary covers the best of both Old and New Delhi: Qutub Minar (UNESCO World Heritage), Humayun's Tomb (UNESCO), India Gate, President's Estate (Rashtrapati Bhavan drive-by), Lodi Garden, the Lotus Temple, Jama Masjid, Red Fort (exterior), and a private rickshaw ride through the spice markets of Chandni Chowk. The route is flexible and can be customised on request.

We offer a range of vehicles to suit your group size: a luxury Sedan (Toyota Etios or similar) for 1–2 guests, a Toyota Innova Crysta SUV for 2–4 guests, a premium Innova or Maruti Ertiga for 5–7, and a Toyota Tempo Traveller for groups of 8–12. All vehicles are air-conditioned, freshly cleaned, and driven by licensed, background-verified chauffeurs.

The standard tour runs for approximately 8 hours covering around 80 km. We typically begin at 9:00 AM and return by 5:00–6:00 PM depending on traffic and the number of sites selected. For airport transit sightseeing tours, we offer shorter 4–5 hour express versions. All timings are fully flexible around your schedule and hotel location.

Yes — all packages include a private English-speaking historian guide who accompanies you throughout the day. Our guides are Ministry of Tourism certified, with deep expertise in Mughal architecture, Rajput history, and Delhi's evolution from Shahjahanabad to the Imperial capital. Language guides in French, Spanish, German, and Japanese can be arranged on request.

Absolutely — the itinerary is fully customisable. If you have a particular interest in Mughal architecture, we can focus on Red Fort, Jama Masjid, Humayun's Tomb, and Purana Qila. For those interested in modern India, we can include Akshardham Temple, the Gandhi Smriti museum, and the National Museum. Simply mention your preferences when booking and we will design accordingly.

Lunch is optional and can be added at booking. We partner with a curated selection of restaurants covering all preferences — a heritage Mughlai feast at Karim's in Old Delhi (established 1913), contemporary Indian cuisine at Indian Accent, or a comfortable rooftop restaurant near Humayun's Tomb. Dietary preferences (vegetarian, vegan, halal, Jain) are all accommodated.

Your private vehicle and guide pick you up directly from your hotel lobby, serviced apartment, or Delhi airport arrival hall (T1 or T3). We cover all areas of Delhi — Connaught Place, Aerocity, Paharganj, Defence Colony, and all major hotel zones. Drop-off is to the same location or a different point of your choice (e.g., another hotel, a railway station, or the airport for onward travel).
