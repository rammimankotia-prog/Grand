COMMON QUERIES

Frequently Asked Questions

How early should we start planning our bespoke trip? ┼

We recommend starting the design process at least 3 to 6 months before your intended travel date, particularly if traveling during peak seasons (October to March) when palace properties like Rambagh Palace and Oberoi Amarvilas fill up rapidly.

Can you arrange private jets and helicopter charters? ┼

Yes. Our Aviation Desk handles all local private charters (turboprops, mid-size jets, and medical evacuation standbys). Chartering enables you to skip hours of highway transits and travel directly between private terminals (FBOs) in Delhi, Agra, Jaipur, and Kashmir.

Do you accommodate special dietary needs and physical restrictions? ┼

Absolutely. Every itinerary is fully bespoke. We coordinate with chefs at all palace hotels to handle gluten-free, vegan, kosher, and traditional Jain dietary menus. For physical restrictions, we provide wheelchair-equipped vehicles, special accessibility guides, and porter assistance.

What is your cancellation and rearrangement policy? ┼

Given the highly tailored nature of our palace bookings, cancellation terms vary by package. However, we offer comprehensive Travel Protection plans that allow for rearrangement and date shifts up to 45 days prior to arrival without losing credit value.
