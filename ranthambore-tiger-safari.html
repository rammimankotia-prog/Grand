Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Ranthambore Tiger Safari — answered by our expert travel curators.

Tiger sightings cannot be guaranteed as they are wild animals in their natural habitat. However, Ranthambore has one of India's highest tiger densities and our expert naturalist guides know the terrain extremely well, giving you the best possible chance of an encounter.

You travel in a private, air-conditioned car (typically a Toyota Innova or similar) with a professional driver. The drive is approximately 6–7 hours covering around 380 km via the Jaipur Highway.

Safaris follow forest department timings — typically at dawn (around 6:00–6:30 AM) and afternoon (around 2:30–3:00 PM). Morning safaris are highly recommended as wildlife activity peaks at sunrise.

Absolutely. The jeep rides are comfortable and the park terrain is manageable. Children find the experience incredibly exciting and educational. We adjust the pace for seniors and families.

Wear muted, earthy colours (avoid bright colours that may disturb wildlife). Bring binoculars, a camera, sunscreen, and a hat. Leave bright perfumes behind — tigers have an acute sense of smell.

Yes — on your departure day, there is an optional visit to the historic Ranthambore Fort, a UNESCO World Heritage Site that sits atop a hill inside the national park with stunning panoramic views.
