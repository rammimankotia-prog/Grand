THE LEGACY

A Vision of Transcendent Travel

Founded with a dream to reshape how the world experiences the rich tapestry of the Indian subcontinent, **Grand Holidays** operates at the intersection of history, art, and hospitality.

Standard itineraries ground travelers in the predictable. Our philosophy—visualized in our gravity-defying concepts—seeks to elevate your journey. We weave together Agra's white marble minarets, Jaipur's windswept screens, Delhi's ancient red battlements, and Kashmir's soaring peaks into a seamless, weightless retreat.

Every partner haveli, private transport cabin, and local guide is hand-selected and curated. We do not just book tours; we design legacies.

OUR FOUNDER

FOUNDER & CHIEF EXPERIENCE DESIGNER

Raman Mankotia

Raman Mankotia established Grand Holidays on a singular premise: luxury travel should be as custom and precise as a masterpiece painting. Combining a deep passion for North Indian heritage architecture with an eye for modern digital design and aesthetics, Raman leads a collective of local historians, destination designers, and logistics curators.

Under his vision, Grand Holidays has grown from a boutique private itinerary desk into a leading global concierge, serving discerning travelers who seek to experience the heritage, royalty, and landscapes of India without friction.
