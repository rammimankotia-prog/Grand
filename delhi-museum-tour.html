Sightseeing Itinerary

10:00 AM

National Museum

Step into the cultural gateway of India. House to a staggering collection of antiquities, you will witness 5,000-year-old relics of the Indus Valley Civilization, exquisite Buddhist murals from Central Asia, and royal jewelry. It is a veritable treasure house of India's golden eras.

11:30 AM

National Rail Museum

Sprawling across 10 lush acres, this fascinating archive chronicles 150 years of India's railway heritage. Marvel at majestic royal saloons, vintage steam locomotives, and the legendary Fairy Queen built in 1855—the best-preserved locomotive of its time.

01:00 PM

International Dolls Museum

A delightful conclusion to your journey, this uniquely captivating collection features over 6,000 intricately crafted dolls from 85 countries, each resplendently dressed in authentic regional costumes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Delhi Museum Tour Tour — answered by our Delhi heritage experts.

Our standard itinerary covers the best of both Old and New Delhi: Qutub Minar (UNESCO World Heritage), Humayun's Tomb (UNESCO), India Gate, President's Estate (Rashtrapati Bhavan drive-by), Lodi Garden, the Lotus Temple, Jama Masjid, Red Fort (exterior), and a private rickshaw ride through the spice markets of Chandni Chowk. The route is flexible and can be customised on request.

We offer a range of vehicles to suit your group size: a luxury Sedan (Toyota Etios or similar) for 1–2 guests, a Toyota Innova Crysta SUV for 2–4 guests, a premium Innova or Maruti Ertiga for 5–7, and a Toyota Tempo Traveller for groups of 8–12. All vehicles are air-conditioned, freshly cleaned, and driven by licensed, background-verified chauffeurs.

The standard tour runs for approximately 8 hours covering around 80 km. We typically begin at 9:00 AM and return by 5:00–6:00 PM depending on traffic and the number of sites selected. For airport transit sightseeing tours, we offer shorter 4–5 hour express versions. All timings are fully flexible around your schedule and hotel location.

Language guides in French, Spanish, German, and Japanese can be arranged on request.

Drop-off is to the same location or a different point of your choice (e.g., another hotel, a railway station, or the airport for onward travel).
