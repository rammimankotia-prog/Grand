Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Golden Triangle Tour — answered by our expert travel curators.

The Golden Triangle Tour covers three iconic cities — Delhi (the historic capital), Agra (home of the Taj Mahal), and Jaipur (the Pink City). Together they form a natural triangle across North India and represent the finest blend of Mughal, Rajput, and colonial heritage.

Depending on your chosen travel mode, you can travel by private luxury chauffeur car (Toyota Innova Crysta or Audi Q7) along the scenic Yamuna Expressway, or upgrade to the Gatimaan / Shatabdi Express train for the Delhi–Agra stretch. All transfers are private and include an on-board assistant.

Yes — all our packages include a private early-morning visit to the Taj Mahal before general tourist crowds arrive. Your personal historian guide provides exclusive context while you experience the monument in near-total tranquillity. VIP gate entry is pre-arranged so there is zero queuing.

Accommodation varies by package tier. Our signature options include the Rambagh Palace in Jaipur (former royal residence of the Maharaja), The Oberoi Amarvilas in Agra (with direct Taj Mahal views from every room), and The Oberoi New Delhi. All are handpicked five-star heritage properties.

Absolutely. We craft family-specific itineraries that include elephant rides at Amber Fort, cooking classes for children, rickshaw rides through Chandni Chowk, and storytelling sessions at the Mehrangarh Fort. Vehicles are configured for comfort and safety for all age groups.

The ideal window is October through March, when North India experiences pleasant weather (15–25°C), clear blue skies over the Taj Mahal, and vibrant cultural festivals. April–June can be hot (up to 45°C), and July–September brings the monsoon. We recommend November and February for the most pleasant experience.

Daily gourmet breakfasts are included in all packages. Curated dinners and select lunches are included in premium tiers, featuring rooftop dining at Taj-view restaurants and palace dining experiences. VIP entry tickets to all major monuments — Taj Mahal, Amber Fort, Qutub Minar, Red Fort — are pre-booked and included.

Every itinerary is entirely bespoke. Popular extensions include Ranthambore National Park (tiger safari), Pushkar (camel fair & Brahma Temple), Udaipur (Lake Palace), or a royal village excursion from Jaipur.
