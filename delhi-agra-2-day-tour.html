Delhi to Agra Itinerary

1

Delhi to Agra

DAY 1
  • 08:00 AM: Start your morning with a delicious breakfast at The Indian Grill Restaurant. Afterward, you will be picked up directly from Hotel Grand Godwin by a Grand Holidays representative.
  • 08:30 AM: Transfer to Agra via a comfortable private drive.
  • 12:00 PM: Arrive in Agra. Check-in to your pre-booked luxury hotel and freshen up.
  • 02:00 PM: Visit the Taj Mahal, one of the greatest ensembles of Mughal times.
  • 04:30 PM: Explore the historic Agra Fort.
  • 06:00 PM: Visit the exquisite Itmad-Ud-Daulah (Baby Taj).
  • 08:00 PM: Enjoy dinner and stay overnight in Agra.
2

Agra to New Delhi

DAY 2
  • 08:00 AM: Wake up to a morning breakfast at your hotel in Agra.
  • 09:30 AM: Check-out and start your drive back to Delhi for sightseeing.
  • 01:00 PM: On arrival in Delhi, explore the Red Fort and visit Raj Ghat.
  • 03:30 PM: Explore Qutub Minar and Humayun's Tomb.
  • 05:30 PM: Drive past India Gate and visit famous shopping centers.
  • 07:00 PM: Conclude the tour with a drop-off and relaxing overnight stay at Hotel Grand Godwin.
