THE CONTEXT

A Dream Transcending Gravity

Regional Synthesis & Physics-Defying Design

Inspired by the surreal grandeur of our hero banner, "Grand Holidays" offers luxury tours that weave the architectural wonders of North India with the absolute majesty of nature.

Here, the red sandstone of Delhi’s historic forts, the intricate pink facades of Jaipur's Hawa Mahal, and the pristine white marble of Agra’s Taj Mahal gently lift and float into the brilliant golden sky. Shimmering pools cascade upwards, reflecting the snow-covered peaks of Kashmir and Himachal Pradesh.
