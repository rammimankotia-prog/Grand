Tour Description

Imagine spending a golden afternoon in New Delhi, where every corner holds a new Italian flavour waiting to be discovered. This isn't just a meal — it's a full sensory journey through the world of Italian cuisine, set against the vibrant backdrop of India's capital city. You'll wake up in the comfort of Hotel Godwin Deluxe in Paharganj, enjoy a leisurely Italian-inspired welcome lunch, and then step out for a 5-hour guided Italian food walk through New Delhi's most exciting dining and deli neighbourhoods.

As the sun sets over the city, you'll sit down to a beautifully crafted, multi-course Italian dinner at Trattoria by Delish Mama — one of New Delhi's most beloved culinary destinations. Think handmade tagliatelle, wood-fired bruschetta, silky tiramisù, and an Italian wine list that transports you straight to Tuscany. This is the kind of evening you'll talk about for years.

Your Italian Day in New Delhi

One beautifully curated day, three distinct acts — a relaxed arrival, a flavour-packed afternoon walk, and a fine dining finale you won't forget.

Arrival

🏨 Check-In & Italian Welcome Lunch — Hotel Godwin Deluxe

Morning · Paharganj, New Delhi

Your Italian food adventure begins the moment you step into Hotel Godwin Deluxe. After a smooth check-in, get settled into your well-appointed room and take a moment to breathe in the rhythm of New Delhi. The hotel is perfectly positioned in Paharganj — close to the city's best neighbourhoods and just minutes from where your culinary walk begins.

Before you head out, enjoy a light Italian-inspired welcome lunch at the hotel's restaurant — think fresh caprese salads, warm focaccia, and a good espresso to set the mood. This is your soft opening act before the real adventure kicks in.

  • 🛎️ Smooth check-in & room refresh
  • 🫙 Italian welcome lunch — caprese, focaccia & espresso
  • 🗺️ Meet your expert food guide for a tour briefing
1:30 PM

🏛️ Connaught Place — The Italian Deli & Cheese Walk

1:30 PM – 2:45 PM · Connaught Place, New Delhi

Your guide leads you into the heart of Connaught Place — Delhi's grand circular market — to explore curated Italian delis and gourmet import stores tucked behind the colonnaded arcades. You'll discover aged Parmigiano-Reggiano, imported Italian olive oils, and hand-cured Prosciutto di Parma that feels worlds away from the bustling streets outside.

Your guide shares stories of how Italian food culture first arrived in Delhi — through embassies, expat communities, and passionate chefs who refused to compromise on authentic ingredients. You'll taste your way through three specialty stops.

  • 🧀 Parmigiano, Pecorino & imported cheese tasting
  • 🫒 Extra-virgin Italian olive oil pour & bread dip
  • 🥩 Prosciutto & salami at an artisan deli
3:00 PM

🫙 Khan Market — Pasta, Sauces & Italian Wine Discovery

3:00 PM – 4:15 PM · Khan Market, New Delhi

A short drive takes you to Khan Market — Delhi's most sophisticated neighbourhood for food shopping and café culture. Here, you'll dive into the world of artisan pasta — from bronze-die-cut rigatoni to squid-ink spaghetti nero — alongside imported Italian passata sauces, tartufo products, and a thoughtfully curated Italian wine section.

Your guide hosts a mini Italian wine education session — comparing a Chianti Classico with a Sicilian Nero d'Avola, and explaining the difference between a Barolo and a Brunello. No wine expertise needed — just an open palate and a sense of curiosity.

  • 🍷 Italian wine pairing session — Chianti, Barolo, Prosecco
  • 🍝 Artisan pasta varieties — tasting & comparison
  • 🛒 Optional: purchase Italian pantry ingredients to take home
4:30 PM

👨‍🍳 Hauz Khas — Pasta-Making Class & Gelato Stop

4:30 PM – 5:45 PM · Hauz Khas Village, New Delhi

The highlight of your afternoon arrives as you enter an intimate studio kitchen in the creative heart of Hauz Khas Village. A skilled Italian pastry chef walks you through the secrets of making fresh pasta from scratch — the exact flour-to-egg ratio, how to feel the dough, and how long to rest it before rolling. You'll get hands-on: rolling, cutting, and shaping your own tagliatelle under expert guidance.

Afterwards, cool down with a scoop (or two) of authentic Italian gelato — a rotating selection of seasonal flavours including pistachio, stracciatella, and fig with honey. A leisurely walk by the scenic Hauz Khas reservoir follows at golden hour.

  • 🍝 Hands-on pasta-making class — tagliatelle from scratch
  • 🍨 Gelato tasting — pistachio, stracciatella & seasonal
  • 🌅 Scenic walk by Hauz Khas reservoir at golden hour
Evening

🍷 Fine Dining Evening — Trattoria by Delish Mama

7:30 PM onwards · Trattoria by Delish Mama, Model Town, New delhi

The day culminates in a beautifully set evening at Trattoria by Delish Mama — New Delhi's most talked-about Italian dining experience. The warm terracotta interiors, flickering candlelight, and the intoxicating aroma of wood-fired garlic bread set the perfect scene for a meal that celebrates everything you've discovered today.

Your curated multi-course dinner begins with antipasti misti — burrata on toasted ciabatta, roasted bell peppers with anchovies, and fresh arancini. The primi piatti features handmade pasta cooked to order: choose from cacio e pepe, amatriciana, or the signature truffle tagliatelle. The secondi brings Milanese-style chicken or seabass in white wine and capers. Finish with a pillowy tiramisù or panna cotta with berry compote — and an Italian digestivo to close.

  • 🥗 Antipasti — burrata, arancini & roasted vegetables
  • 🍝 Handmade pasta course — cacio e pepe or truffle tagliatelle
  • 🐟 Main — Milanese chicken or seabass in white wine
  • 🍮 Dessert — tiramisù & panna cotta
  • 🥂 Welcome Prosecco + optional Italian wine pairing menu
